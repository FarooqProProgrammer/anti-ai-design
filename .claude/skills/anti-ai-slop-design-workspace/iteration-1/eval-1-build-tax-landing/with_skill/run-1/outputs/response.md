Your landing page is ready: `index.html` is one file with no build step. It uses Google Fonts and nothing else.

## How I approached it

- **Who it's for:** a freelancer in Lahore who has been getting Payoneer or Wise payouts for years and is a bit worried about FBR. They need to trust you and understand you more than they need to be impressed.
- **The look:** like a careful accountant's ledger. Warm paper colour, near-black ink, and one accent in banker's green. Muted red only marks the "not registered" numbers, like a correction in the margin.
- **The main idea:** the hero shows a worked example as a ruled ledger, not a stock image. One Upwork payout of USD 2,140 at PKR 280.40 is shown two ways: at 1% (not registered) and at 0.25% (PSEB-registered). The visitor can see they keep **Rs 4,501 more on one payment**, so the page shows your value with real numbers.
- **What I avoided on purpose:** purple gradients, a centred hero with a badge, three icon cards, emoji, glass or glow effects, fake "Sarah J." testimonials, and hype words like "Unlock" and "Seamless".

## What's on the page

1. A strip about the 30 September deadline. It's 5 October, so it speaks to people who just missed it.
2. Hero: a headline, one main button ("Book a free 20-minute review" on WhatsApp) and the ledger example.
3. Five common problems as a numbered list: paying the higher rate, not being on the Active Taxpayers List (ATL), missing PRCs, a wealth statement that doesn't add up, and FBR notices.
4. A table of fixed fees, with a highlighted "First-year set-up" bundle.
5. A timeline from Day 1 to Day 14.
6. A short "why we only take freelancers" note in a letter style.
7. A plain question-and-answer list (not a collapsing accordion).
8. A closing section in green asking them to send one bank statement on WhatsApp.

Type is Spectral for headings, IBM Plex Sans for body text and IBM Plex Mono for figures. Small Urdu section labels are set in Noto Nastaliq. The layout is responsive (checked at 390px and 1366px), focus states are visible, and the HTML is semantic. The design checker found nothing to flag.

  - what you need to qualify for the lower rate
  - the exchange rate in the example

  These rules change every budget. The page already says this in small print, but the numbers themselves need to be right.
