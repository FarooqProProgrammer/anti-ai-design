01 مسئلے

What we see most from freelancers walking in

Nobody is trying to dodge tax. Most people simply set up their Payoneer or Wise account years ago and never connected it to FBR. These are the five problems that come up almost every week.

  1. Paying the higher rate on every payout

    Foreign remittances for IT and IT-enabled services carry a lower final tax if you're registered with PSEB as a freelancer and your bank codes the money as export proceeds. Without that, the bank deducts more — on every single transfer — and it adds up quietly.

  2. Not on the Active Taxpayers List

    No return filed means non-filer rates on cash withdrawals, car registration and property. Filing, even late, is the fix.

  3. Money in, but nothing on paper to explain it

    Payoneer withdrawals sometimes land without the right purpose code, so they look like ordinary transfers. We help you request proceeds realisation certificates (PRCs) from your bank so the income is documented as export earnings.

  4. A wealth statement that doesn't add up

    Bought a car or a plot with freelance savings? Your wealth statement has to reconcile year to year. Gaps are what trigger questions later.

  5. An FBR notice you don't understand

    Usually a notice to file, or a query about a large bank credit. Most are routine if answered properly and on time. Ignoring them is what gets expensive.
