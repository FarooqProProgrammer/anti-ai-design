The questions we get asked every week

"I've never filed. Am I in trouble?"

Usually not, but the longer you wait the more years there are to clean up. We file what's missing, in order, and tell you honestly what penalties apply, if any.

"Why is the bank asking for my NTN?"

Banks, car dealers and property registrars all check the Active Taxpayer List now. Not being on it means higher withholding on almost everything you buy.

"Do I pay tax on Payoneer money?"

Yes, but IT and freelance export income has its own reduced regime. Getting that treatment depends on the money coming in through the right channel and being documented.

"My cousin's accountant just put it under 'salary'."

That is one of the most common mistakes we fix. Wrong income head, wrong rate, and a wealth statement that doesn't reconcile with what's in your bank.

Questions

Straight answers

If yours isn't here, send it on WhatsApp. We reply within a working day.

I earn under the taxable limit. Do I still need to file?

Filing is what puts you on the Active Taxpayer List, and being on it lowers the withholding tax you pay on banking, property and vehicles. For most freelancers that saving alone is more than our fee.

Does it matter if my money comes through Payoneer or Wise?

Both are fine. What matters is that the money arrives in a Pakistani bank account as a foreign remittance, and that the bank can issue a certificate for it. Cash withdrawals from a Payoneer card are harder to document, and we'll explain why on the call.

Is PSEB registration worth it?

For IT and software freelancers it usually is, because it affects the rate applied to your export income. We'll check whether your type of work qualifies before you pay for anything.

When is the deadline?

The tax year runs July to June, and individual returns are normally due by 30 September, though the FBR often extends it. Filing after the deadline is still possible but can carry a penalty and delays ATL status.

Do I need to come to your office?

No. Most clients never visit. Everything can be done over WhatsApp, email and a video call. If you prefer to sit down in person, you're welcome at the office.

Do you handle Punjab sales tax on services?

Yes, if it applies to you. Most individual freelancers exporting their services don't need to register with the Punjab Revenue Authority, but some agencies do. We'll tell you which side of that line you're on.
