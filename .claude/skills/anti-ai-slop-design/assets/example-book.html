01

Brand essence

Help freelancers who earn in dollars keep more of it, legally, without dreading the FBR.

Khata is the Urdu word for a ledger. The brand borrows the calm of a well-kept account book: ruled lines, careful figures, and a double underline under the total.

Audience. Freelancers and remote workers in Pakistan paid via Upwork, Fiverr, Payoneer and Wise; mostly on phones, often anxious about notices and deadlines.

trustworthypreciselocalplain-spoken

We are

  • Calm and exact, like a good accountant
  • Specific — real rates, real deadlines, real fees
  • Bilingual where it helps

We are not

  • A fintech startup
  • Hype-driven or salesy
  • Vague about cost

Rules

  • Headlines in Newsreader, never all caps. Body in Public Sans at 17px+.
  • All money uses tabular figures; right-align amounts in tables.
  • Urdu text gets line-height ≥ 2 and is never squeezed into Latin leading.
  • Max two weights per family on any page.

06

Imagery

Real documents and real people at work in Lahore — paper, phones, payouts. If we can't photograph it honestly, we use a worked example instead.

Do

  • Photograph the actual team and office
  • Show real (anonymised) statements and receipts
  • Use worked number examples as hero images

Don't

  • Stock 'businessman with laptop' photos
  • 3D illustrations, blobs or abstract gradients
  • Fake dashboard mockups

09

Voice & tone

Specific over impressive

Quote the rate, the fee, the deadline. Numbers build more trust than adjectives.

Plain, then precise

Say it simply first, then give the exact legal term in brackets.

Calm about deadlines

Urgency without panic — tell people what to do next.

We say

  • File your 2026 return for Rs 6,500, fixed.
  • Registered with PSEB? Your rate drops to 0.25%.
  • Missed 30 September? Here's what to do this week.

We don't say

  • Unlock your financial freedom
  • Seamless, effortless tax solutions
  • Revolutionizing tax for the next generation
10

Guardrails

What keeps this brand from drifting back into generic AI defaults.

  • No purple/blue gradients, glassmorphism, glows or blurred blobs.
  • No emoji icons, no icon-in-gradient-square feature cards.
  • No fake testimonials or invented client counts.
  • No centered hero + three feature cards template.
  • Never present tax figures without the source year.

Audit any new page with scripts/slop_scan.py and the swap test before shipping.
